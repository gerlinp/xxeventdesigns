Good to Know

Consultation Fee Policy

At XX Event Design, we provide personalized, professional event planning guidance tailored to your vision and needs. To ensure dedicated time and expertise, all initial consultations require a consultation fee.

If you choose to move forward with our event planning services, your consultation fee is applied toward the total cost of your event.

The consultation includes
✓ Event concept and vision discussion
✓ Professional planning advice
✓ Budget and timeline guidance
✓ Recommendations for design, logistics, and next steps
Please note
  • Consultation fees are non-refundable if services are not booked.
  • No planning services begin until the consultation fee is paid.
  • Event dates are not secured until a contract is signed and a deposit is received.
