About Us

Sixteen years of celebrations across New England.

Since 2010, we have been providing services to the New England area for all occasions — including but not limited to Weddings, Birthdays, Baby Showers, First Communions, Sweet 16's, Graduations, Anniversaries, and all matters of events!

Creativity is our middle name

Grown from one event at a time.

XX Event Design prides itself on making sure your event is unique and tailored to your needs. Creativity is our middle name. From Centerpieces, Table Linens, Desserts, Photo Backdrops, and more, we can create your vision just as you imagined it.

We'd rather take on fewer events, done exactly right, than spread ourselves thin. Every celebration on our calendar gets a dedicated lead planner who stays with you from the first conversation to the last dance.

You're only as good as your last event.
